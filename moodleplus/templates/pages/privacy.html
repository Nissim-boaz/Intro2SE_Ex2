Moodle+ is a student project, built for learning. It is not a commercial service.

What we keep

When you sign in with Google, we use your email address to check that you are on the list of users an admin added. We store your name, email and role, and what you do on the site: your courses, attendance, assignments and grades, chat messages, and lesson recordings that lecturers upload.

The microphone

The microphone is used only to check in to attendance. Your phone listens for a short sound code played in the classroom, decodes it right in your browser, and sends only that code. The audio itself is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else. The site uses two outside services: Google, to sign you in, and Google's Gemini, which processes lesson transcripts and, when a lecturer asks for it, assignment submissions.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. A few display preferences, like dark mode, stay in your own browser.
